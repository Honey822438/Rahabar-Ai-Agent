# Campus Shuttle Service

From 1 December 2026 the university runs a free shuttle between the Islamabad campus and the Faizabad metro station. Buses leave the campus gate every 30 minutes from 7:30 am to 6:00 pm, Monday to Friday.

Students must show their university ID card when boarding. The shuttle does not run on public holidays or during the semester break. Complaints go to the transport office in the admin block.
