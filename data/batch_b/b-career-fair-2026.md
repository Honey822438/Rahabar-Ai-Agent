# Career Fair 2026

The annual career fair will take place on 15 January 2027 at the Islamabad campus from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm. More than forty companies from software, telecom and finance are expected.

Final-year students should upload an up-to-date CV to the career services portal by 5 January 2027. Formal dress is required. Students should bring printed copies of their CV and their university ID card.

On-the-spot interviews will be held for internship and graduate positions. Students who receive an offer must inform career services within one week.
