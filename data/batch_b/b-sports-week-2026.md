# Inter-Campus Sports Week 2026

The inter-campus sports week will be held in the second week of December 2026 at the Lahore campus. Events include cricket, football, basketball, table tennis, badminton and chess.

Each campus may register one team per event. Registration closes on 30 November 2026 through the campus sports society. Players must be enrolled students in good academic standing and must carry their university ID card at every match.

Travel for registered teams is arranged by each campus. Winning teams receive trophies, and the best player of each event receives a certificate of merit.
