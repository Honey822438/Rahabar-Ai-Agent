# Computer Lab Safety Rules

1. Food and drinks are not allowed inside computer labs.
2. Students must log out of lab machines before leaving.
3. Report any damaged equipment to the lab engineer on duty.
4. Lab bags must be kept on the racks outside the lab.
5. Installing software on lab machines requires the lab engineer's permission.
