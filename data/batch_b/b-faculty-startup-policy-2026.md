# Faculty Entrepreneurship / Technical Start-ups Policy (revised November 2026)

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines cover full-time faculty members who found, manage, hold equity in, sit on the board of, or receive revenue from a technical start-up that commercialises research conducted at NUCES campuses. This revision replaces the policy dated 30 May 2022.

## 6. Evaluation criteria for a technical start-up

6.1 The faculty member must have published at least one journal paper in a JCR impact-factor journal with NUCES affiliation within **three years** before the date of application (the 2022 policy required two years).

6.2 The faculty member must have been Principal Investigator or Co-Principal Investigator of an externally funded research project within the last five years.

6.3 The start-up must align with ongoing research and degree programmes at the university, and must aim to commercialise emerging technologies.

## 9. Conditions

9.1 The university provides no infrastructure or financial support to the start-up as part of this approval; any support is requested separately through the campus Director.

9.2 The start-up must notify the campus Director and the ORIC office if it employs or pays any university staff or faculty member.

9.3 A workload exemption of **up to 3 credit hours per semester** may be granted by the Dean for the first two semesters after approval (the 2022 policy allowed no exemption).

9.4 Approval is reviewed every **two years**.
