# Northfield Institute of Technology: Academic Regulations (fictional, noise document)

Students at Northfield must keep a cumulative grade point average of at least 2.30 to avoid being placed on academic probation. A student on probation for two terms in a row is dismissed.

Attendance of 75 percent is required in every module. Students below this level receive an X grade and cannot sit the final assessment.

A bachelor's degree at Northfield must be completed within eight years. The summer term lasts six weeks. The normal load is six modules per term, and a student may take a seventh with the registrar's approval.
