# Riverside College Fee Notice 2026 (fictional, noise document)

Tuition at Riverside College is charged at 15,500 per credit hour for undergraduate programmes. The admission fee is 25,000 and is paid once. A refundable security deposit of 10,000 is charged at enrolment.

Students who withdraw within the first week receive a full refund; after the third week no refund is given. A semester freeze costs 3,500 and is allowed for one term only.
